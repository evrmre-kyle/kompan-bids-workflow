How opportunities are captured & cases created

The operational workflow opens with RFP Intake & Opportunity Registration. This playbook details the hands-on mechanics behind that step — finding signals in Starbridge, deciding whether an opportunity already lives in CRM, creating leads and bid cases in Dynamics 365, and spinning up contract cases — captured screen-by-screen from the 2026 B&P SOP.

↖ Returns to “RFP Intake” on the main chart
A
Capture Coordinator — Finding & Creating Leads
From a raw Starbridge signal to a qualified opportunity in CRM
1
Navigate StarbridgeCoordinator

Log in to Starbridge and open Bridges from the left-hand ribbon, then select the BP-US Playground RFP Opportunities bridge. What's listed are all the signals — i.e. RFP opportunities — that might be applicable to Kompan.

2
Vet & prioritize signalsCoordinator

Prioritize signals by RFP Pre-Screening Score, then review each project summary. A higher score doesn't necessarily mean a valid opportunity — vet every signal at a high level before actioning.

  • Use the Ask Starbridge chat to learn more about a project.
  • If no files are linked, the Pre-Screening Score will say so. Identify the signal's source, register for the bid documents, or email support@starbridge.ai / daniel@starbridge.ai with the Buyer Name to have them uploaded on the back end.
3
Assign the signal to yourselfCoordinator

Claim the signal in Starbridge so the team knows it's being worked, then create the link within Dynamics 365 (CRM).

B
Contract Case Creation
Spinning up a contract review case for PC in Dynamics 365
1
Create a Quick caseB&P

Click the “+” sign in the top bar to open Quick Create: Case.

2
Fill the asterisked fieldsB&P

Complete every field marked with an asterisk — Account, Case Title, Group, Type, Assign to Others — and add a Description.

3
Upload contract documents to BoxB&P

Open the Box folder via Opp. Folder (Web). File map: Presales › Bid › Bid Documents. Then share the link on the timeline.

4
Push the flyover to PrepareB&P

Advance the case to the Prepare stage. PC won't see the case until it has been pushed to this stage.

5
Add a Resolve By dateB&P

Set the Resolve By date (and Follow Up By) on the case.

C
Disqualifying a Bid Case
When a case won't be pursued — record it cleanly without moving through the stages
1
Record the disqualificationAnalyst
  • Identify the reasoning for disqualifying and communicate to knowing parties if already discussed.
  • Ensure “Worked By” is assigned to you.
  • Under the Analysis stage, set Bid Disqualified = ‘YES’ and add a Reason Code.
  • Click Resolve Stage (Resolve = ‘YES’). Do not move it through the stages — just Resolve after recording the reason.
Place the reason under the bid case (it does not have to be added under the Opportunity). Cancel the case if no work was required from B&P and an analysis didn't occur. If bid forms were completed, mark “Yes” in the Resolve case stage.
