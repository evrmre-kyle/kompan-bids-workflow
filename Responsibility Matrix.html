Three avenues for B&P engagement

How — and when — to request the Bid & Proposal team, depending on where you are in the lifecycle and who owns the bid.

Pre-Bid
Prequalification Task
Monday.com
Definition

A task-work request that occurs before an RFP has been released.

Example

Preliminary paperwork to become an approved vendor — vendor registrations and pre-qualifications.

Request via
Pre-Bid Task Request Form ↗
Turnaround
~2 weeks
Workflow
  1. Requestor fills out the request form correctly
  2. Request lands in the B&P queue (Monday.com)
  3. B&P rep self-assigns — emails the requestor
  4. B&P rep completes the work — emails the requestor
CRM Case
Bid Management
CRM Dynamics 365
Definition

Bid & Proposal owns the review, planning and execution of our proposal to a customer.

Example

When Kompan is the prime bidder, or a high-value project that requires extensive review.

Request via
“Bid Management” Case
Turnaround
2 weeks min.*
Workflow
  1. Requestor opens a “Bid Management” case off the Opportunity — leave stage as ‘Identify’
  2. B&P self-assigns within 48 hours; analyses documents and schedules a kickoff call to review requirements, plan and timeline
  3. B&P oversees submission and tracks until awarded
⬤ Bid & Proposal owns the bid process
CRM Case
Bid Support
CRM Dynamics 365
Definition

A task-work request that occurs after a bid is released. Sales owns the bid but needs B&P support.

Example

Filling out forms, gathering signatures, or compiling cut sheets. If more than one item is required, B&P may flip the case to “Bid Management”.

Request via
“Bid Support” Case
Turnaround
1–2 weeks**
Workflow
  1. Requestor opens a “Bid Support” case off the Opportunity and gives direction on what's needed
  2. B&P self-assigns and completes the work as directed
  3. B&P uploads to the Box folder and closes the case
⬤ Sales owns the bid process
?Is there an Opportunity and/or Bid Documents?

Yes → open a CRM Case (Bid Management or Bid Support).
No → it's a Pre-Bid Task on Monday.com.

?Manage or support?

Manage = end-to-end ownership — compliance reviews, win strategy and proposal development; takes weeks. Support = a discrete, task-based assist; Sales owns the bid.
